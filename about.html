About SINEKULTURA

SINEKULTURA (from sine, "cinema," and kultura, "culture") is a space to discover Filipino films, especially independent and lesser-known works, and to share what they made you think and feel. It is built for students, film lovers, and anyone curious about Philippine history and everyday life seen through Filipino filmmakers' eyes.

Common terms

  • Sine – cinema, movie
  • Kultura – culture
  • Pelikula – film
  • Bayan – town, nation, people
